# Consent: el-03-szkoda-komunikacyjna

Synthetic call. No real person speaks in it and every name, number and address
in the script is invented.

- **Made with:** ElevenLabs text-to-speech, model `eleven_multilingual_v2`,
  premade voices `eric` (agent, left channel) and `laura` (customer, right
  channel).
- **ElevenLabs plan at generation:** Free.
- **Generated:** 2026-10-30, by the Aftertalk team, from the script in
  `script.json`.
- **Audio in this repository:** none yet. The Free plan's terms do not clearly
  allow redistribution, so the audio stays out until the licence gate of
  item 10 (#18) is cleared, by regenerating on a paid plan if needed. That
  regeneration updates the plan and date above.
